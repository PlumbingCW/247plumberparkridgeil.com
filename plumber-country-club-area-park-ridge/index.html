Country Club Area plumbing at a glance

A named area

The Cook County hazard annex for Park Ridge lists a Country Club Area sewer replacement action.

Club anchor

Park Ridge Country Club is at 636 N Prospect Ave and was established in 1906, according to a golf directory.

Mixed home ages

One local broker says homes in the Country Club area date from 1868 to 2019. That is a sales listing claim, not a city figure.

Mostly houses

Citywide, the community profile shows 82.8 percent owner occupied homes and about 77 percent single family detached.

A residential area around the club, and why sewers come up

The Country Club area is usually tied to Park Ridge Country Club at 636 N Prospect Ave, though no source defines the boundary. Listing sites describe the surrounding neighborhood differently, so no single boundary is documented. We treat the club and Prospect Avenue as the anchor and ask for your street address on every call.

Homes here range widely in age according to a local broker's summary, with houses from 1868 to 2019. We do not assume a pipe material from a year. A plumber has to see the pipe, or a camera has to show it, before anyone knows what it is.

The reason this page leans toward sewers and basements is the city's own paperwork. The Cook County hazard mitigation annex for Park Ridge lists an action called Country Club Area sewer replacement, estimated at 100 million dollars. The city's 2017 sewer study also lists a storage project labeled PRCC Storage. These are plans and estimates from public documents. Ask the city for the current schedule.

Prepare

Four things homeowners can check this month

Sump pump test

Pour water into the pit and watch it start and discharge. See sump pump repair.

Floor drain

Pour a bucket into the basement floor drain and make sure it drains freely and does not smell.

Backwater valve

Ask a plumber whether your sewer line has a backflow device and whether it is working. Overhead sewer work is a larger project.

Sewer line view

A sewer camera inspection shows what the line looks like. It is useful before buying or after a backup.

Water heaters and everyday repairs in larger homes

Not every call here is about flooding. Larger houses often have more fixtures, longer pipe runs and bigger water heaters. A hot water complaint can mean a tank that is failing or a mixing valve problem. Our water heater replacement and water heater repair pages explain what we check first.

If the house has a finished basement, a leak is more costly because walls and floors hide the pipe. Early leak detection can save tearing out more than needed. A meter that moves when every tap is closed is a useful early sign.

What the public record says about flooding in Park Ridge

Park Ridge sits on the Des Plaines River, and the hazard annex says much of its flooding is linked to an antiquated combination sewer system. It records nearly 500 flooded basements on July 23, 2011, and a federal flood declaration in April 2013.

A 2013 Patch report on a Center for Neighborhood Technology study counted 2,062 flood damage claims in ZIP code 60068 from 2007 to 2011, with an average claim of 5,584 dollars. That figure covers the whole ZIP, not the Country Club area.

We do not claim that any single street here floods more than another. Check your own history, your insurance and the city's maps.

Urgent: act now

Before we arrive: sewage or water in the basement

Stay out of standing water if the power is on, and keep children and pets away from sewage.

  1. Do not use sinks, tubs or toilets until the line is clear.
  2. Shut off power to the basement only if it is safe and dry to reach the panel.
  3. Move valuables above the water line.
  4. Photograph everything for the insurer.
  5. Call dispatch and describe the water color and where it enters.
