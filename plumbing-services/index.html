Emergency or scheduled: how to decide

Treat water you cannot stop, sewage coming up through a drain, a leaking water heater and a failed sump pump as emergencies. Those can damage a home within hours, and they are handled ahead of planned work.

Dripping faucets, slow drains, a running toilet and upgrades can wait for a same-day or next-day slot when available. Scheduling them calmly usually costs less and gives the plumber room to do the job well.
