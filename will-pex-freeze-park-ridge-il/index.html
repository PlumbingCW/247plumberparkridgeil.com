Will PEX Plumbing Freeze? Park Ridge, IL Guide

PEX handles a freeze better than copper, but it is not freeze-proof. The tubing is flexible, so it can stretch as water turns to ice, and the Plastics Pipe Institute (PPI) says frozen PEX often does not burst. Tight spaces, fittings and poor installation are where it still fails.

Park Ridge, IL sits in USDA Zone 5b, so this is a winter question here, not a rare one. If a PEX line is frozen right now, open the nearest faucet, warm the pipe gently, and call our 24/7 line if you see any water where it should not be.

What PEX does well in the cold, and where it still fails

The PPI technical report TR-52 credits PEX with three helpful traits: low thermal conductivity, flexibility at very low temperatures, and elasticity. Low conductivity only slows freezing, it does not stop it. The report says the tubing typically expands without cracking or splitting, so a frozen PEX pipe often survives.

The same report is clear about the limits. PEX buried in concrete or tightly packed soil may not expand evenly and can split, and PPI says PEX should not be intentionally frozen. A pipe that came through one cold snap tells you very little about the next one.

Fittings are the part most homeowners worry about, and the PPI report is honest here too. It cites a lab test in which an elbow fitting failed after 23 freeze cycles, which the authors blamed on installation error, while the replacement fitting lasted over 350 cycles. The lesson is that a well-made joint matters as much as the tubing, so any weep at a fitting after a freeze is a repair job, not something to watch.

Where pipes freeze inside a typical house

Freezing is about cold air reaching the pipe, not about what the pipe is made of. These are the usual trouble spots.

Before the first hard freeze: a prevention checklist

  • ✓Disconnect garden hoses and shut off the indoor valve that feeds each outdoor hose bib, then open the bib to drain it.
  • ✓Find your main water shut-off valve and make sure every adult in the house can turn it.
  • ✓Insulate any supply pipe in the garage, crawl space or along an exterior basement wall.
  • ✓Seal visible air gaps around pipes where they pass through walls or the rim joist.
  • ✓Open sink cabinet doors on exterior walls during a deep freeze so room heat reaches the pipes.
  • ✓Let a faucet trickle on the farthest-from-the-main fixture during extreme cold, since moving water is harder to freeze.
  • ✓Never turn the heat fully off if you travel. Keep the thermostat at a steady, moderate setting.

Park Ridge note: Zone 5b winters and who owns the pipe outside

Park Ridge falls in USDA Plant Hardiness Zone 5b, and the Cook County hazard mitigation plan notes that winter storms here can bring more than 10 inches of snow. That means a deep freeze can follow a big storm, so the time to insulate and drain hose bibs is October or November, not the night the temperature drops.

The City of Park Ridge FAQ says the homeowner repairs the water service pipe between the buffalo box and the house. If that buried line freezes or splits, it is a private repair, so water service line repair is a call to a plumber, not to the city.

The same FAQ says that for an after-hours street shutoff, residents call the Police Department non-emergency line, and that shutting off water at the street does not stop the bill. If a pipe is leaking inside, close your own main valve first. It is faster than waiting on a street shutoff.

How to thaw a frozen PEX pipe safely

1

Open a tap

Open the faucet nearest the frozen section so melting ice and steam have somewhere to go.

2

Find the freeze

Look for a section that is cold, frosted, or bulging, usually on an exterior wall or near a vent.

3

Warm gently

Use a hair dryer, warm rags or a heat lamp at a safe distance. PPI warns against open flame, excessive heat and electric resistance thawing devices.

4

Check for leaks

Once water flows, look along the pipe and at every fitting for drips, then keep the faucet running briefly.

PEX or copper in a freeze: honest trade-offs

PEX

  • Can stretch as ice forms, so splits are less common than in rigid metal.
  • Fewer fittings in long runs, which means fewer places for ice to trap pressure.
  • Still vulnerable at fittings and where it is confined, such as inside concrete.
  • Cannot be thawed with open flame or electric resistance devices.

Copper

  • Rigid, so ice expansion tends to split the pipe wall.
  • Conducts cold quickly, so it can freeze faster in a cold wall.
  • Can be thawed more aggressively, though heat near soldered joints still needs care.
  • Splits are often long and obvious once the ice melts.

When a freeze turns into a burst pipe

If you see water spraying, a stain spreading on a ceiling, or a meter that keeps moving with every tap closed, treat it as a leak right away. Shut off the main valve, open a low faucet to drain the lines, and call. Our team handles burst pipe repair around the clock, with same-day appointments when available.

There is one more PPI warning worth taking seriously. PEX that was thawed with too much heat can look fine but be weakened, and PPI says it should be replaced promptly. If a run has frozen more than once, or the tubing looks discolored, kinked or swollen, ask about repiping that section instead of waiting for the next cold snap.

What to do before help arrives

If a pipe has split or is dripping, close the main water shutoff, open a faucet to relieve pressure, and keep power away from wet areas. Repairs follow the Illinois Plumbing Code, 77 Ill. Adm.
