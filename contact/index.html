What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help us send the right plumber with the right equipment.

  • ✓Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • ✓What you see: where the water is, and whether it is clean water or sewage.
  • ✓Whether you can reach and close the main water shutoff.
  • ✓How long it has been happening, and whether it is getting worse.
  • ✓Anything you already tried, such as a plunger or drain cleaner.

Emergency or scheduled visit

Call the 24/7 line for any emergency: a burst pipe, a sewer backup, a leaking water heater or a sump pump that has quit. Active leaks and backups are placed ahead of planned work.

For a non-urgent job such as a faucet upgrade, a water softener or a repipe estimate, call during the day and ask for the earliest same-day or next-day appointment. Same-day appointments are offered when available.
