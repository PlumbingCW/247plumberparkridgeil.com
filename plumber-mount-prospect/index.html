Repeat losses

Mount Prospect note: water, Public Works and permits

Mount Prospect is a village in Cook County with 56,852 residents in the 2020 Census, and 68.7 percent of homes are owner-occupied (Census QuickFacts). Its 2023 water quality report says the water comes from Lake Michigan, is treated by Chicago and reaches the Village through the Northwest Suburban Municipal Joint Action Water Agency. The Village's own Public Works Department runs the distribution system.

Not every address is on the Village system. Its service-line program page excludes Illinois American Water customers, so check your water bill to see who serves you before you call the wrong office. Public Works answers on weekdays during business hours, and after hours the Village points residents to the police non-emergency line.

For plumbing work, the Village's Building and Inspection Services Department issues the permit and has a plumbing inspector. Permit rules vary by job, so we confirm with the Village before work starts.

What homeowners notice first

The common first sign is a floor drain that gurgles or a basement toilet that bubbles during a heavy rain, then clears when the storm passes. That pattern points to the combined main filling up, not a clog in your own pipe. If it happens in dry weather, the blockage is more likely in your house sewer, which is the right job for sewer line cleaning.

Slow drains on every fixture at once, a sewer smell near the cleanout, or wet footprints at the lowest drain are also reasons to call. Storm-driven backups tend to arrive fast and recede, while a house sewer blockage keeps coming back.

Urgent: act now

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak in Mount Prospect

Water and sewage both move quickly through a finished basement. These steps help while our emergency dispatch line is on its way to you.

  1. Shut off the main water valve if a supply pipe is leaking.
  2. Stop using sinks, toilets, the dishwasher and the washer if a drain is backing up.
  3. Turn off power to the wet area at the panel only if the floor around the panel is dry.
  4. Keep children and pets out of any sewage water.
  5. Move belongings off the floor if you can do it safely.
  6. Call our 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher which drain is affected.

The overhead sewer rebate and what it does to your price

The Village's overhead sewer rebate page describes a rebate of up to 50 percent of direct costs, capped at $7,500, for converting a gravity sewer to an overhead one. Only single-family homes qualify, drain tile must be disconnected from the sanitary or combined sewer, and the work needs a sump pit and pump discharging to the yard.

That makes it a plumbing-installation job as much as a repair, and it needs permits and inspections. See our page on plumbing installation for the general process. For your real number, call our 24/7 line.

State rules that apply in Mount Prospect
